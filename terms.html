Welcome to DAMSAM Food Wurld. By ordering food online or reserving tables through our website, you agree to comply with the following terms and conditions.

1. Ordering & Preparation

All meals are prepared fresh to order. Estimated preparation times range between 20 to 35 minutes depending on dish complexity. While we make every effort to deliver within quoted timelines, delivery traffic or weather in Lagos may occasionally cause slight delays.

2. Pricing & Payment

All prices are listed in Nigerian Naira (₦) inclusive of relevant packaging. Delivery fees are calculated separately based on destination zone. Payment may be completed via debit card, direct bank transfer to our official Zenith Bank account, or cash on delivery where available.

3. Cancellations & Table Bookings

Table reservations are complimentary and held for up to 20 minutes from the reserved slot. If you need to modify your booking or order, please contact our desk at +234 803 456 7890 as early as possible.
