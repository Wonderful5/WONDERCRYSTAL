THE ATELIER PROTOCOL

The Bespoke Commission Journey

How we guide an initial concept into a finished architectural heirloom.

01

Spatial Discovery

We analyze your floor plans, natural lighting directions, and architectural palette to establish proportion and silhouette requirements.

02

Material Curation

Select your specific wood slabs, raw Roman travertine blocks, textured Italian bouclés, and hand-waxed leather swatches in our Osun showroom.

03

Atelier Handcrafting

Master cabinetmakers mill, chisel, and hand-rub each piece using traditional joinery and plant-wax finishes in our Osun State workshops.

04

White-Glove Placement

Our specialized delivery artisans position each item precisely according to design drawings, leaving your space spotless and ready for living.
