Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At DAMSAM Food Wurld, accessible from our official website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an online food order or make a table reservation, we may ask for your contact information, including your name, telephone number, email address, delivery street address, and payment method choice. We do not store full credit card numbers on our frontend systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to fulfill your meal preparation, coordinate delivery dispatch with our riders, communicate reservation status, and notify you regarding special offers and updates if you opt in.

3. Contact Information

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at orders@damsamfoodwurld.com or visit us at Plot 14, Admiralty Way, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria.
